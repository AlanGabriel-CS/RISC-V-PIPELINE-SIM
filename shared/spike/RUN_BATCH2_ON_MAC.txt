Batch 2 comparison, ROUND 3 -- run this on the Mac
===================================================

Round 2 already came back 100.00% on all 8 n50/n150/n400 programs --
that's confirmed, nothing to redo there. What's new in round 3: two more
programs, n700_seed700101 and n700_seed700102. n=700 was the one left
out of every earlier round (round 1: X-propagation; round 2: still
wouldn't finish, flagged as needing a waveform). That's now root-caused
and fixed -- a real RTL bug in loop_buffer.sv plus a second, unrelated
bug in gen_program.py's own safety fallback. Full story in
batch2/MANIFEST2.txt's "ROUND 3" section.

Same loop as before, just re-run it -- the n50/n150/n400 dirs will just
re-confirm what round 2 already showed, and the two new n700 dirs are
where the real new information is:

  cd /Volumes/riscv-sim/shared/spike

  for d in batch2/*/; do
    d=${d%/}
    echo "=== $d ==="
    spike -l --log-commits --isa=rv32i --instructions=5000 "$d/program.elf" 2> "$d/spike_trace.log"
    python3 compare_accuracy.py "$d/spike_trace.log" "$d/commit_log.txt" > "$d/comparison_result.txt" 2>&1
    grep ACCURACY "$d/comparison_result.txt"
  done

--instructions=5000 still covers n=700 fine (its real content is
~1994-2038 estimated retirements, well under that), same idea as always:
extra budget just means Spike sits in the repeated epilogue a bit
longer, which compare_accuracy.py already truncates on both sides.

Same note as before: use this folder's spike_log_parser.py /
sv_log_parser.py / compare_accuracy.py, not the rtl/ copies (removed
from the repo since they'd drifted -- this folder's copies are the only
ones that exist).

n700_seed700101/700102 were only validated locally (clean settle, zero
X's) before landing here -- this is genuinely the first time loop_buffer's
fix gets checked against the actual golden model, not just self-checked.
If these come back at anything less than 100%, that's real, new signal --
